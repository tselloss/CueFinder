IMPORTANT: This Cookie Policy complies with GDPR (EU 2016/679), ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC), and Greek data protection legislation.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) when you visit a website. Cookies allow the website to "remember" your actions and preferences.

How They Work:

  • 📝 Stored in your browser
  • 🔄 Sent back to the server on each visit
  • ⏰ Have an expiration date
  • 🗑️ You can delete them at any time

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • ✅ Keep users logged in
  • ✅ Remember your preferences (e.g., language)
  • ✅ Improve website performance
  • ✅ Understand how the platform is used
  • ✅ Protect against fraud and spam

5. Session vs Persistent Cookies

Session Cookies

  • ⏰ Duration: Deleted when you close the browser
  • 🎯 Purpose: Maintain login during browsing
  • 📝 Examples: session_id, csrf_token

Persistent Cookies

  • ⏰ Duration: Remain for a specific period
  • 🎯 Purpose: Remember preferences between visits
  • 📝 Examples: language, auth_token

7. How to Manage Cookies

7.1. Through Our Website:

Cookie Settings
Go to: Settings → Privacy → Cookies
There you can:
✅ Enable/Disable cookie categories
✅ See which cookies are active
✅ Delete all cookies

7.2. Through Your Browser:

Google Chrome:

  1. Settings → Privacy and security
  2. Cookies and other site data
  3. Select "Block third-party cookies" or "Block all cookies"

Firefox:

  1. Options → Privacy & Security
  2. Cookies and Site Data
  3. Select your preferences

Safari:

  1. Preferences → Privacy
  2. Cookies and website data
  3. Select "Block all cookies"

Edge:

  1. Settings → Cookies and site permissions
  2. Manage and delete cookies

7.3. Opt-Out from Analytics:

  • Google Analytics: Google Analytics Opt-out
  • Do Not Track: Enable "Do Not Track" in your browser
Warning: Disabling all cookies may affect website functionality. You may not be able to log in or use certain features.
