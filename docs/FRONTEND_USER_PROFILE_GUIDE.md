# User Profile Settings - Quick Start Guide

## What's New?

You can now save your personal information (phone, email, location, address) once, and it will persist across your entire session. No more re-entering seller information every time you post a cue!

## How to Use

### Access Your Profile
1. Click the **"My Profile"** button in the header (top-right of the page)
2. You'll see two tabs: **Profile Settings** and **My Cues**

### Profile Settings Tab

#### Viewing Your Profile
- Shows your current information
- Fields displayed:
  - **Account Information**: Name, Email, Phone, Location
  - **Address**: Street, City, State/Province, Postal Code, Country

#### Editing Your Profile
1. Click **"Edit Profile"** button
2. Update any fields you want to change
3. Click **"Save Changes"** to save
4. You'll see a success message confirming the update

### My Cues Tab

#### Viewing Your Posted Cues
- Shows all cues you've posted to the marketplace
- Each cue displays:
  - Thumbnail image
  - Title/Brand name
  - Price (in green)
  - Status badge (AVAILABLE or SOLD)
  - Condition tag

#### Managing Your Cues
- **Edit**: (Prepared for future - click to edit cue details)
- **Delete**: Click to remove a cue from the marketplace
  - A confirmation dialog will appear before deletion
  - Deleted cues cannot be recovered

## Responsive Design

The profile works great on all devices:

- **Mobile (iPhone/Android)**: Single-column layout, stacked buttons, easy to read
- **Tablet**: Two-column information grid, horizontal buttons
- **Desktop**: Three-column grid, optimized spacing

Try resizing your browser or opening on different devices!

## Important Notes

- ✅ Your profile information is saved immediately when you click "Save Changes"
- ✅ Your phone, email, location, and address are stored in your user account
- ✅ You only need to set up your profile once
- ⚠️ Deleting a cue will permanently remove it (confirm before deleting!)
- 🔒 Only you can view and edit your own profile

## Tips

1. **Update your profile first** before adding cues - your info will be remembered!
2. **Check your phone number** - make sure it's correct for buyers to contact you
3. **Use your actual location** - helps buyers know where you're shipping from
4. **Review your cues regularly** - manage your inventory from My Cues tab

## Need Help?

If you have issues:
1. Make sure you're logged in (you should see "Signed in as [Your Name]")
2. Check that all required fields are filled (Name, Email at minimum)
3. Try refreshing the page if changes don't save
4. Contact support if problems persist
